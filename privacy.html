Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Joi is a private, self-hosted personal assistant. It has exactly one user: its owner, who is also its developer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data Joi accesses

After the owner signs in with Google and grants permission, Joi can access the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts (read-only).

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform tasks the owner asks for, such as reading and drafting emails, managing calendar events, and working with the owner's documents and spreadsheets. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties. To answer a request, the relevant text may be processed by the AI model provider the owner has chosen, only for that request.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private server, which is protected with key-only SSH access and a firewall. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

Joi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
